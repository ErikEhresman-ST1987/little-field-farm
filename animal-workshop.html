Reuse for another animal or app

The workshop is intentionally independent of Little Field Farm’s game logic.

  1. Duplicate the animal’s layered markup and rename its animal class.
  2. Write the direction and anatomy map before changing any shapes.
  3. Build the large silhouette first; add recognizable features second.
  4. Check both true-size previews and simplify anything that becomes muddy.
  5. Transfer only the approved animal markup and CSS into the destination app.

Design gate: if the animal is not recognizable without labels, the silhouette needs another pass before animation or decorative markings are added.
